Know your power cost for the next three years.

Prices have been rising faster than inflation and demand is at record levels. A fixed rate takes the swings out of your budget.

Prices will not rise in every market or every year. A fixed rate is about certainty, not a promise of lower prices.

How it works

  1. 1

    Buy

    We buy hedged power through an institutional wholesale partner.

  2. 2

    Fix

    You get one fixed rate for the term, with a transparent margin on top.

  3. 3

    Settle

    We manage the market settlements, so you deal with one counterparty.

What if market prices fall?

You still pay your fixed rate. That is the trade for certainty.

What credit support do you need?

We assess credit before signing. For large loads we may ask for a deposit or a parent guarantee.

Who holds the retail licence?

Not confirmed yet. The plan is to begin through a licensed partner while applying for our own.

How it earns

A margin on every kWh sold under fixed-rate contracts; fees for bespoke structures such as green tariffs; later, demand response, storage advice and equipment finance.

Years 1 to 3 goal

40 to 60 active contracts and over $10M a year in gross margin, with its own retail licence in the main markets and 20 or more consultants.
